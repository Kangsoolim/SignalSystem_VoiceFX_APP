V2.5 Final Targeted Tuning Design
=================================

Core rule:
Naturalness first + minimum perceptual category separation.

Female direction:
- Low-pitched source: stronger positive pitch floor.
- Mid source: moderate lift.
- High source: no forced upward floor.
- Formant/envelope reinforcement scales with low-source gap.

Child direction:
- Every adult source receives a source-dependent minimum positive pitch shift.
- Low source receives more pitch lift.
- High source still receives a small positive lift.
- Child formant/envelope direction stays stronger than female.
- No fixed output F0 is used.

Unchanged:
- male planner
- elder planner
- noise engine
- VAD-aware denoiser
- result preservation
- batch processing
- module switches
- AM path
