V2.5 NATURAL ADAPTIVE FINAL - DESIGN NOTE

Goal
----
Natural-sounding four-class voice transformation with source-dependent planning.

Planner inputs
--------------
F0 median
pitch range in semitones
F0 coefficient of variation
voiced ratio
pitch confidence
spectral centroid
low-band energy ratio
high-band energy ratio
dynamic range
energy onset rate proxy
rhythm interval CV proxy

Planner policy
--------------
1. Broad class F0 bands are soft priors, not fixed targets.
2. Distance to the nearest band edge defines direction.
3. A tanh budget function limits the global pitch shift smoothly.
4. Wide expressive pitch contours receive a smaller shift budget.
5. Timing, pauses, and speaking rate are preserved.
6. Rhythm proxies only tune compressor release timing.
7. Low-band support is source-dependent to avoid the previous "fat male" result.
8. Envelope warping is mild and bounded.
9. File processing uses full-utterance shiftPitch with phase locking and formant preservation when available.
10. Aggressive HQ cepstral warping and elder micro-delay processing are not used in the active final chain.

Soft style priors
-----------------
male:
  F0 band 105-195 Hz
  max downward budget 5.3 st
  mild downward envelope direction

female:
  F0 band 170-290 Hz
  max upward budget 5.0 st
  mild upward envelope direction

child:
  F0 band 245-380 Hz
  max upward budget 6.0 st
  stronger but still bounded upward envelope direction

elder:
  F0 band 100-240 Hz
  moderate pitch movement only
  mild warm tilt, breathiness, and low-depth continuous roughness

These bands are engineering priors for the course project and are not demographic definitions.
